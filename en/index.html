Real situations from hospitality operations

Do you recognise yourself in any of these?

Five problems we see in almost every hospitality operation. The data is in the system. The decisions are late.

01

You have 30 reports in your POS. And not a single decision.

Summary, KPIs, revenue centres, KDS, delivery, timesheets — each in a different PDF, and nobody puts them together. Problems show up two or three months after they started.

02

You lose money on refunds and don't know who approves them.

In a real operation: RON 239,000 (≈ €48,000) in refunds in one year, two thirds authorised by a single person. Nobody had looked.

03

The kitchen is late and you don't know which station.

KDS shows you orders in real time, but it doesn't tell you that 60% of pizzas leave the kitchen over the target time. Your customers feel it before you do.

04

Orders are falling year on year and nobody raises the flag.

−7.2% orders versus last year means 16,400 lost orders. Without a month-by-month year-on-year comparison, you only see it when it's too late.

05

Your loyalty programme: is it making money or eating it?

Points granted, redeemed, expired, topped up manually — in separate reports. The points balance is a liability that grows (+23% in one month) without you seeing it.

All this data already exists in your systems. What's missing is the one page that puts it together.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the fastest way to reach you?

WhatsApp, at +40 772 295 031 — we usually reply within minutes, 09:00–18:00, seven days a week. For a structured conversation, book a 30-minute Google Meet video call directly in our calendar.

Do we need to change our POS or ERP system?

No. The module connects to Breeze, ExpresSoft or Charisma ERP & HCM and reads the data you already have. We change nothing in your existing systems — we bring them together on one page.

How long until we see the first results?

At most 10 working days from signing the contract to your first dashboard, including an audit of recent months and the first recommendations. The deadline is guaranteed in the contract.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the number of locations, the systems connected and the complexity of the analysis. After the initial conversation and the free analysis you receive a clear proposal: an implementation project plus a monthly maintenance subscription. No hidden costs.

Who has access to our data?

Only the people you designate. We sign a non-disclosure agreement and a GDPR data-processing agreement, and the dashboard is private, with login and secure access.

We have several locations or several companies. Does it work?

Yes. The module consolidates multiple locations and profit centres, compares them with each other and shows where each one performs.

Do you work in Sweden or with companies outside Romania?

Yes. We operate in Romania and Sweden and work in Romanian, English and Swedish. Meetings can take place on Google Meet or WhatsApp video, wherever you are.

What happens after delivery?

Maintenance and support on a subscription basis: updates, new reports, monitoring — plus consulting for the next automations in your company. For emergencies we are available 24/7.
